While green technologies are gaining momentum, a recent <a href="https://www.eco-business.com/press-releases/asia-pacific-at-a-climate-inflection-point-new-kpmg-google-report-calls-for-coordinated-action-to-scale-greentech-ecosystems/">report</a> shows they aren’t scaling fast enough to keep up with the region’s rising environmental risks.</p><p data-block-key="5tvnv">To help innovators tackle these environmental challenges, we’re launching an inaugural Google DeepMind Accelerator program in APAC focused on “AI for the Planet.”</p><p data-block-key="d2bje">This three-month program is designed for startups, research teams and nonprofits across the region to use frontier AI to solve problems in nature, climate, agriculture, energy and more.
