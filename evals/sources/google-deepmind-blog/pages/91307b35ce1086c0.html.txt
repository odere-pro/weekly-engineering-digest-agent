    <div class="rich-text"><p data-block-key="tsg8h">Last year, <a href="https://deepmind.google/models/gemini-image/">Nano Banana</a> brought Gemini's intelligence to image generation and editing. Since then, it’s helped millions of people restore old photos, design from sketches and visualize ideas in ways that weren’t possible before. From the start we built Gemini to be natively multimodal from the ground up, and now we’re taking the next step.</p><p data-block-key="c9gpk">We’re introducing <a href="http://deepmind.google/models/gemini-omni">Gemini Omni</a>, where Gemini’s ability to reason meets the ability to create. Omni is our new model that can create anything from any input — starting with video. With Omni, you can combine images, audio, video and text as input and generate high-quality videos grounded in Gemini's real-world knowledge. You can also easily edit your videos through conversation.</p><p data-block-key="fa9bo">Today, we’re rolling out the first model in the Omni family: Gemini Omni Flash, to the Gemini app, Google Flow and YouTube Shorts. In time we will support output modalities like image and audio. Here’s some of what makes Omni special:</p><h2 data-block-key="afri3">Edit your videos through conversation</h2><p data-block-key="e4bgk">Gemini Omni gives you an easier way to edit video — with natural language. Every instruction builds on the last. Your characters stay consistent, the physics hold up and the scene remembers what came before.</p><p data-block-key="3f3fr"><b>Transform the world around you.</b> Change specific things, or change everything. Your video becomes the starting point for something you never could have filmed yourself.</p></div>
