    <div class="rich-text"><h2 data-block-key="rewjm">Developing Argon responsibly and safely</h2><p data-block-key="c5q0l">Safety and security are foundational to model development at Google. Because Argon is a highly capable model, we collaborated with a variety of third-party partners on measurement of certain model capabilities and safeguards. Gemini 4 Argon represents our most comprehensive safety and security stack yet:</p><ul><li data-block-key="1q3tr"><b>Misuse &amp; jailbreak defenses:</b> The same coding and scientific capabilities that help defenders patch hospital software or accelerate bioengineering also require strong safeguards against malicious actors. On coding benchmarks where models have no incentive to hold back, shortcut attempts dropped to just 2% on Gemini 4 Argon, our best performance compared to previous Gemini models.</li><li data-block-key="7k26i">Second, as a defense-in-depth layer, we built an automated misalignment monitor that reads the agent’s step-by-step reasoning and actions to catch and block unintended workarounds in real time.
