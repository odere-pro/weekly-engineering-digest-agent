            <h3 id="notable-changes"><a href="#notable-changes">Notable Changes</a></h3>
            <p>
              The known issue relating to <code>Buffer.allocUnsafe</code> incorrectly zero-filling
              buffers has now been addressed and returns uninitialized memory as documented in the
              <a
                href="https://nodejs.org/docs/latest-v24.x/api/buffer.html#static-method-bufferallocunsafesize"
                ><code>Buffer.allocUnsafe</code></a
              >
              documentation.
            </p>
