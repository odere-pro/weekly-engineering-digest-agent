            <h4 id="http2-network-inspection-support-in-nodejs">
              <a href="#http2-network-inspection-support-in-nodejs"
                >HTTP/2 Network Inspection Support in Node.js</a
              >
            </h4>
            <p>
              Node.js now supports inspection of HTTP/2 network calls in Chrome DevTools for
              Node.js.
            </p>
