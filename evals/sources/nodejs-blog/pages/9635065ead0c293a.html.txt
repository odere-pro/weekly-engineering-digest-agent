            <h2 id="2025-10-15-version-2500-current-rafaelgss">
              <a href="#2025-10-15-version-2500-current-rafaelgss"
                >2025-10-15, Version 25.0.0 (Current), @RafaelGSS</a
              >
            </h2>
            <p>
              Node.js 25 is here! We have upgraded V8 to <strong>14.1</strong>, bringing major
              <code>JSON.stringify</code> performance improvements, built-in
              <code>Uint8Array</code> base64/hex conversion, and ongoing WebAssembly and JIT
              pipeline optimizations.
            </p>
            <p>
              This release doubles down on secure-by-default apps and web-standard APIs: the
              permission model gains <code>--allow-net</code>, Web Storage is enabled by default,
              and <code>ErrorEvent</code> is now a global.
            </p>
            <p>
              We’ve also removed or finalized long-deprecated APIs such as SlowBuffer, and added
              quality-of-life improvements like a portable compile cache and JSPI for WebAssembly.
            </p>
