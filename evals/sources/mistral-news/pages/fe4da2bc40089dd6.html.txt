However, in the rush to attempt to put AI to use, it all too often seems that AI is something that happens <em>to</em> people and countries, an inevitability beyond their influence that leaves them at the mercy of closed, opaque systems designed and operated by distant, behemoth corporations. </p><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">At Mistral AI, we’ve believed from the very beginning in <a href="/about" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline font-semibold">frontier AI in your hands</a>, i.e. in giving people and organizations the ability to shape and determine the role of AI in their future. That’s why we&#39;re excited to introduce <a href="/solutions/ai-for-citizens" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline font-semibold">AI for Citizens</a>, a collaborative initiative to help States and public institutions strategically harness AI <em>for</em> their people by transforming public services, catalyzing innovation, and ensuring competitiveness.<br><br>We&#39;re already working with governments, defense forces, public sector agencies, and educational institutions across the world—<a href="/solutions/ai-for-citizens" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline font-semibold">including France, Luxembourg, Singapore, the Netherlands, England, Switzerland, and many more</a>—to put this vision into practice.</p><div class="h-10 md:h-30 pb-10 flex items-center"><hr class="absolute left-0 w-full text-border-primary"></div><h2 class="text-h3 font-mistral text-current mb-5 pb-5 border-b border-border-primary not-first:mt-16">The risks of “one size fits all” AI</h2><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">Today’s AI landscape is dominated by closed, opaque offerings with limited flexibility, encouraging a “one size fits all” approach to AI. As public institutions look for solutions to help them implement AI strategies, those offerings come with significant limitations and risks.</p><ul class="mb-10 pl-5 flex flex-col gap-2 list-disc"><li><p class="text-body-large text-current"><h5 class="text-h6 font-mistral text-current">Closed and inflexible</h5>Offerings from the dominant vendors are largely proprietary, black-box offerings that are difficult or impossible to tailor and customize, severely limiting transparency, flexibility, and effectiveness while leaving countries captive to the priorities, architectures, and approaches of outside vendors.</p></li><li><p class="text-body-large text-current"><h5 class="text-h6 font-mistral text-current">Dependence on dominant vendors</h5>A small number of dominant providers currently provide the lion’s share of AI technology and expertise, sapping local innovation, creating monopolistic vendor lock-in, and transferring significant economic value to foreign players.</p></li><li><p class="text-body-large text-current"><h5 class="text-h6 font-mistral text-current">Subject to geopolitical risks</h5>Dependence on vendors and technologies that are subject to control and regulation by superpowers creates significant risk and uncertainty as AI increasingly becomes a strategic lever in trade, regulatory, and competitive battles.</p></li><li><p class="text-body-large text-current"><h5 class="text-h6 font-mistral text-current">Lack of control</h5>Dependence on foreign technology can severely limit the ability to ensure that data transfer, processing, and governance are aligned with national requirements for safety, privacy, and protection.</p></li><li><p class="text-body-large text-current"><h5 class="text-h6 font-mistral text-current">AI diversity</h5>Reliance on AI models, algorithms, and applications tuned and trained by a few dominant foreign vendors can easily result in AI that does not serves the full range of languages, cultures, norms, and perspectives globally.</p></li></ul><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">To sum it up, &quot;one size fits all&quot; AI offerings cannot meet the tactical nor strategic needs of regions and countries. Understanding local languages, incorporating local context, and aligning with unique priorities in each region and country requires AI solutions that are designed and tailored to fit the specific use cases and requirements of those regions, countries and their people. Not only that, but “one size fits all” AI offerings take control of the impact of AI on their future out of the hands of countries and their citizens.</p><div class="h-10 md:h-30 pb-10 flex items-center"><hr class="absolute left-0 w-full text-border-primary"></div><h2 class="text-h3 font-mistral text-current mb-5 pb-5 border-b border-border-primary not-first:mt-16">Putting your AI future in your hands</h2><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">The AI for Citizens initiative provides an alternative to “one size fits all” AI solutions. As a leader in open technologies for AI, Mistral is uniquely able to build on open technologies to foster innovation and prevent lock-in.</p><h3 class="text-h4 font-mistral text-current mb-5 not-first:mt-12">Deployment control and choice</h3><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">Choose self-hosting for full control, utilize AI datacenters operated by Mistral AI and its local partners to get easier access to cutting-edge infrastructure, or take advantage of SaaS offerings and serverless APIs from Mistral AI.</p><h3 class="text-h4 font-mistral text-current mb-5 not-first:mt-12">Data sovereignty</h3><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">Mistral AI solutions allow you to ensure that data is hosted within sovereign boundaries to maintain control, security, and compliance to national regulations.</p><h3 class="text-h4 font-mistral text-current mb-5 not-first:mt-12">Bespoke research and development</h3><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">Implement customized research and development programs, including co-training and verticalization of models optimized to meet local language, cultural, and use case needs with enhanced capabilities.</p><h3 class="text-h4 font-mistral text-current mb-5 not-first:mt-12">Roadmap visibility</h3><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">Gain access to valuable roadmaps from Mistral AI to guide your development and deployment.</p><div class="h-10 md:h-30 pb-10 flex items-center"><hr class="absolute left-0 w-full text-border-primary"></div><h2 class="text-h3 font-mistral opportunities to deliver on key strategic priorities for AI:</p><ul class="mb-10 pl-5 flex flex-col gap-2 list-disc"><li><p class="text-body-large text-current"><h5 class="text-h6 font-mistral text-current">Transform government services with AI</h5>Establish centers of excellence to build local expertise within government to help government agencies improve their services and create new services using AI.</p></li><li><p class="text-body-large text-current"><h5 class="text-h6 font-mistral text-current">Spark research and development innovation</h5>Implement customized research and local entities such as universities, public and private research centers.</p></li><li><p class="text-body-large text-current"><h5 class="text-h6 font-mistral text-current">Foster a vibrant AI We want to enable governments to become self-reliant with the most powerful technology of our time, delivering better services, responding to citizen needs, and preserving their unique heritage for future generations.
