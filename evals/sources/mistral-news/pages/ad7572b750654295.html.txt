              <section class="relative w-full">
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Mistral AI brings the strongest open generative models to the developers, along
                  with efficient ways to deploy and customise them for production.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  We&#39;re opening a beta access to our
                  <a href="https://console.mistral.ai" class="underline font-semibold"
                    >first platform services</a
                  >
                  today. We start simple: la plateforme serves three chat endpoints for generating
                  text following textual instructions and an embedding endpoint. Each endpoint has a
                  different performance/price tradeoff.
                </p>
                <h4 class="text-h5 font-mistral text-current mb-5">Generative endpoints</h4>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  The two first endpoints, mistral-tiny and mistral-small, currently use our two
                  released open models; the third, mistral-medium, uses a prototype model with
                  higher performances that we are testing in a deployed setting.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  We serve instructed versions of our models. We have worked on consolidating the
                  most effective alignment techniques (efficient fine-tuning, direct preference
                  optimisation) to create easy-to-control and pleasant-to-use models. We pre-train
                  models on data extracted from the open Web and perform instruction fine-tuning
                  from annotations.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  <strong>Mistral-tiny</strong>. Our most cost-effective endpoint currently serves
                  Mistral 7B Instruct v0.2, a new minor release of Mistral 7B Instruct. Mistral-tiny
                  only works in English. It obtains 7.6 on MT-Bench. The instructed model can be
                  downloaded
                  <a
                    href="https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.2"
                    class="underline font-semibold"
                    >here</a
                  >.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  <strong>Mistral-small</strong>. This endpoint currently serves our newest model,
                  Mixtral 8x7B, described in more detail in our
                  <a href="/news/mixtral-of-experts/" class="underline font-semibold">blog post</a>.
                  It masters English/French/Italian/German/Spanish and code and obtains 8.3 on
                  MT-Bench.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  <strong>Mistral-medium</strong>. Our highest-quality endpoint currently serves a
                  prototype model, that is currently among the top serviced models available based
                  on standard benchmarks. It masters English/French/Italian/German/Spanish and code
                  and obtains a score of 8.6 on MT-Bench. The following table compare the
                  performance of the base models of Mistral-medium, Mistral-small and the endpoint
                  of a competitor.
                </p>

                  <figure class="mb-10">

                <h4 class="text-h5 font-mistral text-current mb-5">Embedding endpoint</h4>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Mistral-embed, our embedding endpoint, serves an embedding model with a 1024
                  embedding dimension. Our embedding model has been designed with retrieval
                  capabilities in mind. It achieves a retrieval score of <strong>55.26</strong> on
                  MTEB.
                </p>
                <h4 class="text-h5 font-mistral text-current mb-5">API specifications</h4>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Our API follows the specifications of the popular chat interface initially
                  proposed by our dearest competitor. We provide a Python and Javascript client
                  library to query our endpoints. Our endpoints allow users to provide a system
                  prompt to set a higher level of moderation on model outputs for applications where
                  this is an important requirement.
                </p>
                <h4 class="text-h5 font-mistral text-current mb-5">
                  Ramping up from beta access to general availability
                </h4>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Anyone can
                  <a href="https://console.mistral.ai" class="underline font-semibold">register</a>
                  to use our API as of today as we progressively ramp up our capacity. Our business
                  team can help qualify your needs and accelerate access. Expect rough edges as we
                  stabilise our platform towards fully self-served availability.
                </p>
                <h4 class="text-h5 font-mistral text-current mb-5">Acknowledgement</h4>

                  and working alongside us to make a sparse mixture of experts compatible with
