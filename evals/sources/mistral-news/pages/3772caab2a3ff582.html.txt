pt-6"><section class="relative w-full"><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">Today, we announce Mistral 3, the next generation of Mistral models. Mistral 3 includes three state-of-the-art small, dense models (14B, 8B, and 3B) and Mistral Large 3 – our most capable model to date – a sparse mixture-of-experts trained with 41B active and 675B total parameters. All models are released under the Apache 2.0 license. Open-sourcing our models in a variety of compressed formats empowers the developer community and puts AI in people’s hands through distributed intelligence.</p><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">The Ministral models represent the best performance-to-cost ratio in their category. At the same time, Mistral Large 3 joins the ranks of frontier instruction-fine-tuned open-source models.</p><h2 class="text-h3 font-mistral text-current mb-5 pb-5 border-b border-border-primary not-first:mt-16">Mistral Large 3: A state-of-the-art open model</h2><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10"><figure class="mb-10"><img src="/_astro/98aeee04-e1c3-43b7-b90e-c51da84d5e56_ZdKC34.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f" srcset="/_astro/98aeee04-e1c3-43b7-b90e-c51da84d5e56_Z2nOFiR.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f 400w, /_astro/98aeee04-e1c3-43b7-b90e-c51da84d5e56_Zy8xz5.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f 768w, /_astro/98aeee04-e1c3-43b7-b90e-c51da84d5e56_19WeLG.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f 1280w, /_astro/98aeee04-e1c3-43b7-b90e-c51da84d5e56_1QzOEG.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f 1920w" alt="Chart Base Models (1)" sizes="(max-width: 640px) 400px, (max-width: 1024px) 768px, (max-width: 1440px) 1280px, 1920px" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1905" height="1242" class="w-full"></figure></p><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10"><figure class="mb-10"><img src="/_astro/bdf27a12-76fd-4e62-be9b-938f14288a9a_ZqfLXe.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f" srcset="/_astro/bdf27a12-76fd-4e62-be9b-938f14288a9a_2nx2LT.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f 400w, /_astro/bdf27a12-76fd-4e62-be9b-938f14288a9a_ZRgcMM.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f 768w, /_astro/bdf27a12-76fd-4e62-be9b-938f14288a9a_Z11BVkz.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f 1280w, /_astro/bdf27a12-76fd-4e62-be9b-938f14288a9a_FMdgy.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f 1920w" alt="3 Model Performance Comparison (instruct)" sizes="(max-width: 640px) 400px, (max-width: 1024px) 768px, (max-width: 1440px) 1280px, 1920px" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1346" height="1115" class="w-full"></figure></p><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">Mistral Large 3 is one of the best permissive open weight models in the world, trained from scratch on 3000 of NVIDIA’s H200 GPUs. Mistral Large 3 is Mistral’s first mixture-of-experts model since the seminal Mixtral series, and represents a substantial step forward in pretraining at Mistral. After post-training, the model achieves parity with the best instruction-tuned open-weight models on the market on general prompts, while also demonstrating image understanding and best-in-class performance on multilingual conversations (i.e., non-English/Chinese).</p><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">Mistral Large 3 debuts at #2 in the OSS non-reasoning models category (#6 amongst OSS models overall) on the <a href="https://lmarena.ai/leaderboard/text" class="underline font-semibold">LMArena leaderboard</a>.</p><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10"><figure class="mb-10"><img src="/_astro/4626af3d-7554-4d50-9c0e-041fe7111ece_Z1qTh5k.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f" srcset="/_astro/4626af3d-7554-4d50-9c0e-041fe7111ece_2tXbbF.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f 400w, /_astro/4626af3d-7554-4d50-9c0e-041fe7111ece_ZKwOSt.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f 768w, /_astro/4626af3d-7554-4d50-9c0e-041fe7111ece_Z3bpfz.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f 1280w, /_astro/4626af3d-7554-4d50-9c0e-041fe7111ece_DraCq.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f 1920w" alt="Lm Arena Chart Ml3" sizes="(max-width: 640px) 400px, (max-width: 1024px) 768px, (max-width: 1440px) 1280px, 1920px" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1905" height="1242" class="w-full"></figure></p><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">We release both the base and instruction fine-tuned versions of Mistral Large 3 under the Apache 2.0 license, providing a strong foundation for further customization across the enterprise and developer communities. A reasoning version is coming soon! </p><h3 class="text-h4 font-mistral text-current mb-5 not-first:mt-12">Mistral, NVIDIA, vLLM &amp; Red Hat join forces to deliver faster, more accessible Mistral 3</h3><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">Working in conjunction with vLLM and Red Hat, Mistral Large 3 is very accessible to the open-source community. We’re releasing a checkpoint in NVFP4 format, built with <a href="https://github.com/vllm-project/llm-compressor" class="underline font-semibold">llm-compressor</a>. This optimized checkpoint lets you run Mistral Large 3 efficiently on Blackwell NVL72 systems and on a single 8×A100 or 8×H100 node using <a href="https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm" class="underline font-semibold">vLLM</a>.</p><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">Delivering advanced open-source AI models requires broad optimization, achieved through a <a href="https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/mistral-frontier-open-models/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline font-semibold">partnership with NVIDIA</a>. All our new Mistral 3 models, from Large 3 to Ministral 3, were trained on NVIDIA Hopper GPUs to tap high-bandwidth HBM3e memory for frontier-scale workloads. NVIDIA’s extreme co-design approach brings hardware, software, and models together. NVIDIA engineers enabled efficient inference support for <a href="https://github.com/NVIDIA/TensorRT-LLM" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline font-semibold">TensorRT-LLM</a> and <a href="https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline font-semibold">SGLang</a> for the complete Mistral 3 family, for efficient low-precision execution.</p><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">For Large 3’s sparse MoE architecture, NVIDIA integrated state-of-the-art Blackwell attention and MoE kernels, added support for prefill/decode disaggregated serving, and collaborated with Mistral on speculative decoding, enabling developers to efficiently serve long-context, high-throughput workloads on GB200 NVL72 and beyond. On the edge, delivers optimized deployments of the Ministral models on <a href="http://nvidia.com/en-us/products/workstations/dgx-spark/" class="underline font-semibold">DGX Spark</a>, <a href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai-on-rtx/" class="underline font-semibold">RTX PCs and laptops</a>, and <a href="https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/autonomous-machines/embedded-systems/jetson-orin/" class="underline font-semibold">Jetson devices</a>, giving developers a consistent, high-performance path to run these open models from data center to robot.</p><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">We are very thankful for the collaboration and want to thank vLLM, Red Hat, and NVIDIA in particular.</p><h2 class="text-h3 font-mistral text-current mb-5 pb-5 border-b border-border-primary not-first:mt-16">Ministral 3: State-of-the-art intelligence at the edge</h2><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10"><figure class="mb-10"><img src="/_astro/ea1fcc83-5bad-400e-b63a-35c8a8c0bf9c_ZEaRWe.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f" srcset="/_astro/ea1fcc83-5bad-400e-b63a-35c8a8c0bf9c_Z1yPeeQ.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f 400w, /_astro/ea1fcc83-5bad-400e-b63a-35c8a8c0bf9c_YL9Gp.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f 768w, /_astro/ea1fcc83-5bad-400e-b63a-35c8a8c0bf9c_ZPEcw5.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f 1280w, /_astro/ea1fcc83-5bad-400e-b63a-35c8a8c0bf9c_Z2tYriJ.webp?dpl=6ad1d7b6946342e26a4d2b4f 1920w" alt="4 Gpqa Diamond Accuracy" sizes="(max-width: 640px) 400px, (max-width: 1024px) 768px, (max-width: 1440px) 1280px, 1920px" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1726" height="1062" class="w-full"></figure></p><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">For edge and local use cases, we release the Ministral 3 series, available in three model sizes: 3B, 8B, and 14B parameters. Furthermore, for each model size, we release base, instruct, and reasoning variants to the community, each with image understanding capabilities, all under the Apache 2.0 license. When married with the models’ native multimodal and multilingual capabilities, the Ministral 3 family offers a model for all enterprise or developer needs.</p><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">Furthermore, Ministral 3 achieves the best cost-to-performance ratio of any OSS model. In real-world use cases, both the number of generated tokens and model size matter equally. The Ministral instruct models match or exceed the performance of comparable models while often producing an order of magnitude fewer tokens. </p><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">For settings where accuracy is the only concern, the Ministral reasoning variants can think longer to produce state-of-the-art accuracy amongst their weight class - for instance 85% on AIME ‘25 with our 14B variant.</p><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10"> </p><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10"> </p><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10"> </p><h2 class="text-h3 font-mistral text-current mb-5 pb-5 border-b border-border-primary not-first:mt-16">Available Today</h2><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">Mistral 3 is available today on <a href="https://console.mistral.ai/home" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline font-semibold">Mistral AI Studio</a>, <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2025/12/mistral-large-3-ministral-3-family-available-amazon-bedrock/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline font-semibold">Amazon Bedrock</a>, Azure Foundry, Hugging Face (<a href="https://huggingface.co/collections/mistralai/mistral-large-3" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline font-semibold">Large 3</a> &amp; <a href="https://huggingface.co/collections/mistralai/ministral-3" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline font-semibold">Ministral</a>), <a href="https://modal.com/docs/examples/ministral3_inference" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="underline font-semibold">Modal</a>, IBM WatsonX, OpenRouter, Fireworks, Unsloth AI,and Together AI. In addition, coming soon on NVIDIA NIM and AWS SageMaker.</p><h3 class="text-h4 font-mistral text-current mb-5 not-first:mt-12">One more thing… customization with Mistral AI</h3><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">For organizations seeking tailored AI solutions, Mistral AI offers <a href="https://mistral.ai/solutions/custom-model-training" class="underline font-semibold">custom model training services</a> to fine-tune or fully adapt our models to your specific needs. Whether optimizing for domain-specific tasks, enhancing performance on proprietary datasets, or deploying models in unique environments, our team collaborates with you to build AI systems that align with your goals. For enterprise-grade deployments, custom training ensures your AI solution delivers maximum impact securely, efficiently, and at scale.</p><h3 class="text-h4 font-mistral text-current mb-5 not-first:mt-12">Get started with Mistral 3</h3><p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">The future of AI is open.
