                  At Mistral AI, we believe the future of artificial intelligence is open,
                  collaborative, and built for everyone. Today, we’re proud to announce our
                  participation as a founding member in the
                  <a
                    href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-launches-nemotron-coalition-of-leading-global-ai-labs-to-advance-open-frontier-models"
                    class="underline font-semibold"
                    >NVIDIA Nemotron Coalition</a
                  >, a groundbreaking global initiative uniting leading AI labs to advance open,
                  frontier-level foundation models. As part of this strategic partnership, Mistral
                  AI and NVIDIA plan to co-develop frontier open-source AI models, combining Mistral
                  AI’s specialized model architecture and full-stack AI platform with NVIDIA’s
                  compute resources, model-development tools, and synthetic-data generation
                  pipelines. As a founding member of the NVIDIA Nemotron Coalition, Mistral AI will
                  contribute its proprietary training techniques, multimodal capabilities, and
                  enterprise-grade fine-tuning tools—while leveraging NVIDIA’s compute, tools &amp;
                  technologies to scale training and optimization. This builds off prior scientific
                  collaborations together with NVIDIA, including the development of the
                  <a href="/news/mistral-nemo" class="underline font-semibold">Mistral Nemo</a>
                  model.
                </p>
                <h2
                  class="text-h3 font-mistral text-current mb-5 pb-5 border-b border-border-primary not-first:mt-16"
                >
                  Why open collaboration matters
                </h2>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Open models are the cornerstone of democratized AI. As part of this commitment,
                  today Mistral AI is releasing <a
                    href="/news/mistral-small-4"
                    class="underline font-semibold"
                    >Mistral Small 4</a
                  >
                  to empower developers, researchers, and organizations worldwide to build,
                  specialize, and innovate without barriers (read more
                  <a href="/news/mistral-small-4" class="underline font-semibold">here</a>).
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  By joining the NVIDIA Nemotron Coalition, we’re taking another major step toward
                  shaping a future where AI is transparent, accessible, and tailored to diverse
                  needs.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  “Open frontier models are how AI becomes a true platform,” said Arthur Mensch,
                  cofounder and CEO of Mistral AI. “Together with NVIDIA, we will take a leading
                  role in training and advancing frontier models at scale. By shaping the
                  capabilities of these systems from the ground up, we can help establish a global
                  foundation for AI that empowers developers to build the next generation of
                  applications.”
                </p>
                <h2
                  class="text-h3 font-mistral text-current mb-5 pb-5 border-b border-border-primary not-first:mt-16"
                >
                  What this means for the AI community
                </h2>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  The coalition’s first initiative is a base model, trained on NVIDIA DGX Cloud,
                  which will underpin the upcoming NVIDIA Nemotron 4 family. These models will be
                  open-sourced, providing a shared foundation for post-training and specialization.
                  For Mistral AI, this aligns perfectly with our mission: to put AI in everyone’s
                  hands.
                </p>
                <h3 class="text-h4 font-mistral text-current mb-5 not-first:mt-12">
                  Key benefits:
                </h3>
                <ul class="mb-10 pl-5 flex flex-col gap-2 list-disc">
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Shared expertise: By pooling resources and knowledge, we reduce duplication
                      and accelerate progress across the AI ecosystem.
                    </p>
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Customization: Organizations can fine-tune models for their unique domains,
                      regions, and use cases.
                    </p>
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Control: Open models support local control and compliance, ensuring AI works
                      for everyone, everywhere.
                    </p>
                  </li>
                </ul>
                <h2
                  class="text-h3 font-mistral text-current mb-5 pb-5 border-b border-border-primary not-first:mt-16"
                >
                  Mistral AI’s role in the coalition
                </h2>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  As a founding member, Mistral AI is contributing large-scale model development and
                  multimodal capabilities, helping to define state of the art in the open model
                  ecosystem.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Mistral AI brings to the NVIDIA Nemotron Coalition cutting-edge model
                  architectures, deep expertise in model customization through pre-training,
                  post-training, and fine-tuning, and a proven track record in building specialized,
                  high-efficiency models that enterprises already rely on. Together, with NVIDIA,
                  we’re making training more efficient across the AI ecosystem and providing
                  developers with a shared, open foundation to build upon—faster and at scale.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Our collaboration with NVIDIA and other coalition members reflects a shared
                  commitment to:
                </p>
                <ul class="mb-10 pl-5 flex flex-col gap-2 list-disc">
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Transparency: Open-sourcing models, data, and frameworks for global access.
                    </p>
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Collaboration: Fostering a community where innovation is collective, not
                      siloed.
                    </p>
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Impact: Enabling developers to build the next wave of AI applications on a
                      robust, open foundation.
                    </p>
                  </li>
                </ul>
                <h2
                  class="text-h3 font-mistral text-current mb-5 pb-5 border-b border-border-primary not-first:mt-16"
                >
                  The road ahead
                </h2>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  The NVIDIA Nemotron Coalition is just the beginning. Together, we’re setting a new
                  standard for how AI is developed and deployed, one that prioritizes openness,
                  collaboration, and real-world impact. Stay tuned for updates.
                </p>
