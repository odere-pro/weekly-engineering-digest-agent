                  A significant share of le Chat usage comes from students and professionals who use
                  it for learning, research, and work. To help better facilitate these use cases,
                  we’ve augmented le Chat’s training knowledge with internet search.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Here’s one of our marketing teammates using it to assess the AI market for

                <h5 class="text-h6 font-mistral text-current"><strong>Canvas</strong></h5>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Canvas is a new interface that pops up in your le Chat window when you need to go
                  beyond conversations and into ideation. It enables you to collaborate with Mistral
                  LLMs on shared outputs, enabling 0-1 creation and ideation for knowledge work.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  No longer restricted to bidirectional conversations, Canvas enables you to
                  directly modify, edit or transform content aided by the powerful reasoning
                  capabilities of LLMs.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Here’s a video of our product team creating a react mockup of a product metrics
                  dashboard in preparation for the launch of Canvas.
                </p>

                  You can use Canvas to create documents, presentations, code, mockups… the list
                  goes on. As you can see, you’re able to modify its contents in place without
                  regenerating responses, version your drafts, and preview your designs.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Canvas allows for infinite creative ideation for any team.
