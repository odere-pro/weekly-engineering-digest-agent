              <section class="relative w-full">
                <h3 class="text-h4 font-mistral text-current mb-5 not-first:mt-12">
                  Many prototypes. Few systems in production.
                </h3>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Enterprise AI teams have built dozens of prototypes—copilots, chat interfaces,
                  summarization tools, internal Q&amp;A. The models are capable, the use cases are
                  clear, and the business appetite is there.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  What’s missing is a reliable path to production and a robust system to support
                  much of it. Teams are blocked not by model performance, but by the inability to:
                </p>
                <ul class="mb-10 pl-5 flex flex-col gap-2 list-disc">
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Track how outputs change across model or prompt versions
                    </p>
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Reproduce results or explain regressions
                    </p>
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Monitor real usage and collect structured feedback
                    </p>
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Run evaluations tied to their own domain-specific benchmarks
                    </p>
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Fine-tune models using proprietary data, privately and incrementally
                    </p>
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Deploy governed workflows that satisfy security, compliance, and privacy
                      constraints
                    </p>
                  </li>
                </ul>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  As a result, most AI adoption stalls at the prototype stage. Models get hardcoded
                  into apps without evaluation harnesses. Prompts get tuned manually in Notion docs.
                  Deployments run as one-off scripts. And it’s difficult to tell if accuracy
                  improved or got worse. There’s a gap between the pace of experimentation and the
                  maturity of production primitives.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  In talking to hundreds of enterprise customers, we have discovered that the real
                  bottleneck is the lack of a system to turn AI into a reliable, observable, and
                  governed capability.
                </p>
                <h2
                  class="text-h3 font-mistral text-current mb-5 pb-5 border-b border-border-primary not-first:mt-16"
                >
                  How to close the loop from prompts to production.
                </h2>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Operationalizing AI, therefore, requires infrastructure that supports continuous
                  improvement, safety, and control—at the speed AI workflows demand.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  The core requirements we consistently hear from enterprise AI teams include:
                </p>
                <ul class="mb-10 pl-5 flex flex-col gap-2 list-disc">
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Built-in evaluation: Internal benchmarks that reflect business-specific
                      success criteria (not generic leaderboard metrics). 
                    </p>
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Traceable feedback loops: A way to collect real usage data, label it, and turn
                      it into datasets that drive the next iteration.
                    </p>
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Provenance and versioning: Across prompts, models, datasets, and judges, with
                      the ability to compare iterations, track regressions, and revert safely.
                    </p>
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Governance: Built-in audit trails, access controls, and environment boundaries
                      that meet enterprise security and compliance standards.
                    </p>
                  </li>
                  <li>
                    <p class="text-body-large text-current">
                      Flexible deployment: The ability to run AI workflows close to their systems,
                      across hybrid, VPC, or on-prem infrastructure, and migrate between them
                      without re-architecting.
                    </p>
                  </li>
                </ul>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Today, most teams build this piecemeal. They repurpose tools meant for DevOps,
                  MLOps, or experimentation. But the LLM stack has new abstractions. Prompts ship
                  daily. Models change weekly. Evaluation is real-time and use case-specific.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Closing that loop from prompts to production is what separates teams that
                  experiment with AI from those that run it as a dependable system.
                </p>
                <h2
                  class="text-h3 font-mistral text-current mb-5 pb-5 border-b border-border-primary not-first:mt-16"
                >
                  Introducing Mistral AI Studio: The Production AI Platform
                </h2>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Mistral AI Studio brings the same infrastructure, observability, and operational
                  discipline that power Mistral’s own large-scale systems—now packaged for
                  enterprise teams that need to build, evaluate, and run AI in production.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">

                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  At Mistral AI, we operate AI systems that serve millions of users across complex
                  workloads. Building and maintaining those systems required us to solve the hard
                  problems: how to instrument feedback loops at scale, measure quality reliably,
                  retrain and deploy safely, and maintain governance across distributed
                  environments.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  AI Studio productizes those solutions. It captures the primitives that make
                  production AI systems sustainable and repeatable—the ability to observe, to
                  execute durably, and to govern. Those primitives form the three pillars of the
                  platform: Observability, Agent Runtime, and AI Registry.
                </p>
                <h3 class="text-h4 font-mistral text-current mb-5 not-first:mt-12">
                  Observability
                </h3>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Observability in AI Studio provides full visibility into what’s happening, why,
                  and how to improve it. The Explorer lets teams filter and inspect traffic, build
                  datasets, and identify regressions. Judges, which can be built and tested in their
                  own Judge Playground, define evaluation logic and score outputs at scale.
                  Campaigns and Datasets automatically convert production interactions into curated
                  evaluation sets. Experiments, Iterations, and Dashboards make improvement
                  measurable, not anecdotal.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  With these capabilities, AI builder teams can trace outcomes back to prompts,
                  prompts back to versions, and versions back to real usage—closing the feedback
                  loop with data, not intuition.
                </p>

                <h3 class="text-h4 font-mistral text-current mb-5 not-first:mt-12">
                  Agent Runtime
                </h3>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  The Agent Runtime is the execution backbone of AI Studio. It runs every agent,
                  from simple single-step tasks to complex multi-step business flows, with
                  durability, transparency, and reproducibility.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Each agent operates inside a stateful, fault-tolerant runtime built on Temporal,
                  which guarantees consistent behavior across retries, long-running tasks, and
                  chained calls. The runtime manages large payloads, offloads documents to object
                  storage, and generates static graphs that make execution paths auditable and easy
                  to share.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Every execution emits telemetry and evaluation data that flow directly into
                  Observability for measurement and governance. AI Studio supports hybrid,
                  dedicated, and self-hosted deployments so enterprises can run agents wherever
                  their infrastructure requires while maintaining the same durability, traceability,
                  and control.
                </p>

                <h3 class="text-h4 font-mistral text-current mb-5 not-first:mt-12">AI Registry</h3>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  The AI Registry is the system of record for every asset across the AI
                  lifecycle—agents, models, datasets, judges, tools, and workflows.<br />It tracks
                  lineage, ownership, and versioning end to end. The Registry enforces access
                  controls, moderation policies, and promotion gates before deployment. It
                  integrates directly with Observability (for metrics and evaluations) and with the
                  Agent Runtime (for orchestration and deployment).<br />This unified view enables
                  true governance and reuse: every asset is discoverable, auditable, and portable
                  across environments.
                </p>

                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Together, these pillars form the production fabric for enterprise AI.<br />AI
                  Studio connects creation, observation, and governance into a single operational
                  loop—the same system discipline that lets Mistral run AI at scale, now in the
                  hands of enterprise teams.
                </p>
                <h2
                  class="text-h3 font-mistral text-current mb-5 pb-5 border-b border-border-primary not-first:mt-16"
                >
                  Go from experimentation to production, on your terms.
                </h2>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Enterprises are entering a new phase of AI adoption. The challenge is no longer
                  access to capable-enough models—it’s the ability to operate them reliably, safely,
                  and at scale.That shift demands production infrastructure built for observability,
                  durability, and governance from day one.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Mistral AI Studio represents that next step: a platform born from real operational
                  experience, designed for teams that want to move past pilots and run AI as a core
                  system.<br />It unifies the three production pillars—Observability, Agent Runtime,
                  and AI Registry—into one closed loop where every improvement is measurable and
                  every deployment accountable.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  With AI Studio, enterprises gain the same production discipline that powers
                  Mistral’s own large-scale systems:
                </p>

                  This is how AI moves from experimentation to dependable operations—secure,
                  observable, and under your control.

                  If your organization is ready to operationalize AI with the same rigor as software
                  systems, sign up for the private beta of AI Studio.
