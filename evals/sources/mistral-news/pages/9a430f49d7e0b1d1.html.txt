                  the cost and expertise required for customizing generative AI models at scale.

                  services, we&#39;ve got you covered.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Fine-tuning is a powerful technique for customizing and improving the performance
                  of LLMs, providing better responses, flexibility and efficiency to specific
                  applications. When tailoring a smaller model to suit specific domains or use
                  cases, it offers a way to match the performance of larger models, reducing
                  deployment costs and improving application speed.
                </p>
                <h2
                  class="text-h3 font-mistral text-current mb-5 pb-5 border-b border-border-primary not-first:mt-16"

                  Tailor Mistral models at home, on la Plateforme, and with the team
                </h2>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  We&#39;re proud to announce three different entry points for specialising Mistral
                  AI models.

                <h3 class="text-h4 font-mistral text-current mb-5 not-first:mt-12">
                  Open-source fine-tuning SDK for Mistral models
                </h3>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  For developers who want to fine-tune Mistral&#39;s open-source models on their
                  infrastructure, we&#39;ve released
                  <a
                    href="https://github.com/mistralai/mistral-finetune"
                    class="underline font-semibold"
                    >mistral-finetune</a
                  >, a lightweight and efficient codebase for fine-tuning.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Our codebase is built on the LoRA training paradigm, which allows for
                  memory-efficient and performant fine-tuning. With mistral-finetune, you can
                  fine-tune all our open-source models on your infrastructure without sacrificing
                  performance or memory efficiency.
                </p>
                <h3 class="text-h4 font-mistral text-current mb-5 not-first:mt-12">
                  Serverless fine-tuning services on la Plateforme
                </h3>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  To further facilitate fine-tuning without infrastructure hassle, we&#39;re
                  introducing new fine-tuning services on la Plateforme. These services leverage
                  Mistral&#39;s unique fine-tuning techniques, refined through extensive research
                  and development, to allow fast and cost-effective model adaptation and effective
                  deployment of fine-tuned models. It&#39;s a new step in our mission to expose
                  advanced science methods to AI application developers. We use LoRA adapters under
                  the hood to prevent forgetting base model knowledge and allow for efficient
                  serving.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10"></p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">

                  evaluation metric is an normalized internal benchmark very similar to the MTBench
                  evaluation (1 being the reference to full Fine-tuning of Mistral Small).
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Traditionally, fine-tuned models have required a significant investment for
                  enterprises. Our expertise in the field enables us to offer fine-tuning in a
                  highly efficient way, which translates into lower costs for both training and
                  serving.
                </p>
                <p class="text-body-large text-text-primary mb-10">
                  Today, our fine-tuning services are compatible with Mistral 7B and Mistral Small.
                  Thus, users who are leveraging one of these models can immediately use our API to
                  customise our models effectively to meet their specific needs. We will add new
                  models to our finetuning services over the coming weeks.
                </p>

                  Get started with by registering on
                  <a href="https://console.mistral.ai/" class="underline font-semibold"

                  approach enables the creation of highly specialized and optimized models for their
                  particular domain. We propose advanced techniques such as continuous pretraining
                  to include proprietary knowledge within the model weights themselves.
