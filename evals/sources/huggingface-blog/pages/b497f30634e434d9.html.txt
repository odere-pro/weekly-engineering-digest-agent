<blockquote>
<p><!--[--><!--[0--><div class="absolute -left-12 bottom-0 top-0 z-30 not-prose hidden lg:block"><div class="sticky top-4 flex"><div class="h-7 pt-[0.175rem]"><span class="peer" tabindex="0"><button class="select-none text-gray-400 hover:cursor-pointer hover:text-gray-800 dark:text-gray-500 dark:hover:text-gray-400"> <!----></button></span> <div class="invisible w-0 -translate-x-8 -translate-y-6 overflow-hidden rounded-xl border bg-white transition-transform hover:visible hover:w-52 hover:translate-x-0 peer-focus-within:visible peer-focus-within:w-52 peer-focus-within:translate-x-0"> </div></div></div></div><!--]--><!--]--><strong>Evaluate PP-OCRv6 online, then integrate lightweight, production-ready OCR with PaddlePaddle, Transformers, or ONNX Runtime backend.</strong></p>
</blockquote>
<p>PP-OCRv6 is the latest generation of PaddleOCR’s universal OCR model family. It is designed for real-world text detection and recognition across documents, screenshots, multilingual images, digital displays, industrial labels, and scene text.</p>
<p><a href="https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/652b2e9166313ebb6197e706/BB9bToA0xHZ8Xu5cvBGx0.jpeg" rel="nofollow"><img src="https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/652b2e9166313ebb6197e706/BB9bToA0xHZ8Xu5cvBGx0.jpeg" alt="ppocrv6_det_vis"></a></p>
<p>The model family scales from <strong>1.5M to 34.5M parameters</strong>, with three tiers: <strong>tiny</strong>, <strong>small</strong>, and <strong>medium</strong>. The medium and small tiers support <strong>50 languages</strong>, including Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, and 46 Latin-script languages. Try PP-OCRv6 online quickly: <a href="https://huggingface.co/spaces/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv6_Online_Demo">PP-OCRv6 Online Demo</a>.</p>
<p><a href="https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/652b2e9166313ebb6197e706/Rwhy6dk3g8xb6eyVFL9R_.jpeg" rel="nofollow"><img src="https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/652b2e9166313ebb6197e706/Rwhy6dk3g8xb6eyVFL9R_.jpeg" alt="ocrv6_models"></a></p>
<p>On PaddleOCR’s official in-house multi-scenario OCR benchmarks, <strong>PP-OCRv6_medium</strong> reaches <strong>86.2% detection Hmean</strong> and <strong>83.2% recognition accuracy</strong>. Compared with PP-OCRv5_server, it improves text detection by <strong>+4.6 percentage points</strong> and text recognition by <strong>+5.1 percentage points</strong>.</p>
<p><a href="https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/652b2e9166313ebb6197e706/xYqS-wDYHDE7cUQQdkcqD.png" rel="nofollow"><img src="https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/652b2e9166313ebb6197e706/xYqS-wDYHDE7cUQQdkcqD.png" alt="v6acc_opt"></a></p>
<p>PP-OCRv6 focuses on a practical OCR need: producing accurate, structured text outputs with small models and flexible deployment options. For a deeper discussion of why specialized OCR models remain useful in the VLM era, see our previous blog: <a href="https://huggingface.co/blog/baidu/ppocrv5">PP-OCRv5 on Hugging Face: A Specialized Approach to OCR</a>.</p>
<hr>
<h2 class="relative group flex items-baseline">
	<a id="whats-new-in-pp-ocrv6" class="block pr-1.5 text-lg md:absolute md:p-1.5 md:opacity-0 md:group-hover:opacity-100 md:right-full" href="#whats-new-in-pp-ocrv6" rel="nofollow">
		<span class="header-link"> </span>
	</a>
	<span>
		What’s new in PP-OCRv6
	</span>
</h2>
<p>PP-OCRv6 introduces architecture, training, and data improvements across detection and recognition. The main design goal is to improve OCR accuracy while keeping model sizes suitable for different deployment settings.</p>
<h3 class="relative group flex items-baseline">
	<a id="three-model-tiers" class="block pr-1.5 text-lg md:absolute md:p-1.5 md:opacity-0 md:group-hover:opacity-100 md:right-full" href="#three-model-tiers" rel="nofollow">
		<span class="header-link"> </span>
	</a>
	<span>
		Three model tiers
	</span>
</h3>
<p>PP-OCRv6 provides three model tiers, covering different model sizes and OCR accuracy levels.</p>
<div class="max-w-full overflow-auto">
	<table>
		<thead><tr>
<th>Model</th>

<td><strong>1.5M params</strong></td>
<td>80.6%</td>
<td>73.5%</td>

<tr>

<td><strong>7.7M params</strong></td>
<td>84.1%</td>
<td>81.3%</td>

<h3 class="relative group flex items-baseline">
	<a id="pplcnetv4-backbone" class="block pr-1.5 text-lg md:absolute md:p-1.5 md:opacity-0 md:group-hover:opacity-100 md:right-full" href="#pplcnetv4-backbone" rel="nofollow">
		<span class="header-link"> </span>
	</a>
	<span>
		PPLCNetV4 backbone
	</span>
</h3>
<p>PP-OCRv6 uses <strong>PPLCNetV4</strong> as a unified backbone for text detection and text recognition.</p>
<p>For developers, the main benefit is consistency across the model family. The tiny, small, and medium tiers are not unrelated models; they are part of the same OCR family and share a common architectural direction.</p>
<p><a href="https://internal-api-drive-stream.feishu.cn/space/api/box/stream/download/authcode/?code=MzQ2ODgzNWViOTJhYjMzZDUxNTMyY2RlMzdhMDAwZmNfZWJmZGY5NTljOWJlY2YyODVhYzg0N2NhNTk3MjQwMTRfSUQ6NzY1MjcxNjg0NDE1OTMyMzA5N18xNzgxODE5MjkwOjE3ODE5MDU2OTBfVjM" rel="nofollow"><img src="https://internal-api-drive-stream.feishu.cn/space/api/box/stream/download/authcode/?code=MzQ2ODgzNWViOTJhYjMzZDUxNTMyY2RlMzdhMDAwZmNfZWJmZGY5NTljOWJlY2YyODVhYzg0N2NhNTk3MjQwMTRfSUQ6NzY1MjcxNjg0NDE1OTMyMzA5N18xNzgxODE5MjkwOjE3ODE5MDU2OTBfVjM" alt="Image"></a></p>
<h3 class="relative group flex items-baseline">
	<a id="replkfpn-for-text-detection" class="block pr-1.5 text-lg md:absolute md:p-1.5 md:opacity-0 md:group-hover:opacity-100 md:right-full" href="#replkfpn-for-text-detection" rel="nofollow">
		<span class="header-link"> </span>
	</a>
	<span>
		RepLKFPN for text detection
	</span>
</h3>
<p>Text detection is the first stage of the OCR pipeline. Detection quality affects the crops sent to the recognizer, and poor crops often lead to poorer recognition.</p>
<p>PP-OCRv6 upgrades the detection module with <strong>RepLKFPN</strong>, a lightweight large-kernel feature pyramid network designed for multi-scale text detection while keeping inference efficient.</p>
<p>This is relevant for real-world OCR inputs, where text may be small, dense, rotated, low-resolution, or embedded in complex backgrounds.</p>
<p><a href="https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/652b2e9166313ebb6197e706/Unp8cz-s3c4jSMl71qIFf.png" rel="nofollow"><img src="https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/652b2e9166313ebb6197e706/Unp8cz-s3c4jSMl71qIFf.png" alt="ppocrv6_det_pip_ori"></a></p>
<h3 class="relative group flex items-baseline">
	<a id="encoderwithlightsvtr-for-recognition" class="block pr-1.5 text-lg md:absolute md:p-1.5 md:opacity-0 md:group-hover:opacity-100 md:right-full" href="#encoderwithlightsvtr-for-recognition" rel="nofollow">
		<span class="header-link"> </span>
	</a>
	<span>
		EncoderWithLightSVTR for recognition
	</span>
</h3>
<p>For text recognition, PP-OCRv6 uses <strong>EncoderWithLightSVTR</strong>. It combines local context modeling with global attention to improve recognition quality on challenging text crops.</p>
<p>The recognition improvements are especially relevant for multilingual text, screen text, industrial characters, special symbols, dense text, and noisy image regions.</p>
<p><a href="https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/652b2e9166313ebb6197e706/YLhR7a1-BdP62o0o_zZl1.png" rel="nofollow"><img src="https://cdn-uploads.huggingface.co/production/uploads/652b2e9166313ebb6197e706/YLhR7a1-BdP62o0o_zZl1.png" alt="rec"></a></p>
<h3 class="relative group flex items-baseline">
	<a id="unified-multilingual-ocr" class="block pr-1.5 text-lg md:absolute md:p-1.5 md:opacity-0 md:group-hover:opacity-100 md:right-full" href="#unified-multilingual-ocr" rel="nofollow">
		<span class="header-link"> </span>
	</a>
	<span>
		Unified multilingual OCR
	</span>
</h3>
<p>The medium and small tiers support <strong>50 languages</strong> in one model family, covering Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, and 46 Latin-script languages.</p>
<p>This helps reduce the need for separate OCR models across common multilingual OCR scenarios.</p>
<hr>
<h2 class="relative group flex items-baseline">
	<a id="quick-start-with-paddleocr" class="block pr-1.5 text-lg md:absolute md:p-1.5 md:opacity-0 md:group-hover:opacity-100 md:right-full" href="#quick-start-with-paddleocr" rel="nofollow">
		<span class="header-link"> </span>
	</a>
	<span>
		Quick start with PaddleOCR
	</span>
</h2>
<p>Install PaddleOCR:</p>
<pre><code class="language-Bash">pip install paddleocr
</code></pre>
<p>Run OCR with Paddle Infernece(Default backend):</p>
<pre><code class="language-Python"><span class="hljs-keyword">from</span> paddleocr <span class="hljs-keyword">import</span> PaddleOCR

<span class="hljs-comment"># Model: PP-OCRv6_medium(Default)</span>
<span class="hljs-comment"># Backend: Paddle Inference(Default)</span>
ocr = PaddleOCR(
    use_doc_orientation_classify=<span class="hljs-literal">False</span>,
    use_doc_unwarping=<span class="hljs-literal">False</span>,
    use_textline_orientation=<span class="hljs-literal">False</span>,
)
result = ocr.predict(<span class="hljs-string">"https://paddle-model-ecology.bj.bcebos.com/paddlex/imgs/demo_image/general_ocr_002.png"</span>)

<span class="hljs-keyword">for</span> res <span class="hljs-keyword">in</span> result:
    res.<span class="hljs-built_in">print</span>()
    res.save_to_img(<span class="hljs-string">"output"</span>)
    res.save_to_json(<span class="hljs-string">"output"</span>)
</code></pre>
<p>The OCR result can be saved as visualization images and structured JSON output. The structured output can then be used by downstream systems such as document parsing, search, extraction, RAG, analytics, or agent workflows.</p>
<hr>
<h2 class="relative group flex items-baseline">
	<a id="available-inference-backends" class="block pr-1.5 text-lg md:absolute md:p-1.5 md:opacity-0 md:group-hover:opacity-100 md:right-full" href="#available-inference-backends" rel="nofollow">
		<span class="header-link"> </span>
	</a>
	<span>
		Available inference backends
	</span>
</h2>
<p>PP-OCRv6 can be used with multiple inference backends through PaddleOCR. <strong>PaddleOCR 3.7</strong> provides a unified inference-engine interface, where <code>engine</code> selects the underlying runtime and related configuration can be passed through the pipeline or module API.</p>
<div class="max-w-full overflow-auto">
	<table>
		<thead><tr>
<th><strong>Backend</strong></th>
<th><strong>Description</strong></th>
</tr>

		</thead><tbody><tr>
<td><strong>Transformers</strong></td>
<td>Hugging Face / PyTorch-oriented inference path for supported PaddleOCR models</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>ONNX Runtime</strong></td>
<td>Portable inference path for ONNX-based deployment environments</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td><strong>Paddle Inference</strong></td>
<td>Native Paddle inference format</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
	</table>
</div>
<p>For Hugging Face users, PaddleOCR supports running selected OCR and document parsing models with a Transformers backend. This can be enabled with:</p>
<pre><code class="language-Python">engine=<span class="hljs-string">"transformers"</span>
</code></pre>
<p>For more details on how the Transformers backend works in PaddleOCR, see:</p>
<p><a href="https://huggingface.co/blog/PaddlePaddle/paddleocr-transformers">PaddleOCR: Running OCR and Document Parsing Tasks with a Transformers Backend</a></p>
<p>Run PP-OCRv6 example with Transformer Backend:</p>
<pre><code class="language-Python">
<span class="hljs-keyword">from</span> paddleocr <span class="hljs-keyword">import</span> PaddleOCR

<span class="hljs-comment"># Model: PP-OCRv6_medium(Default)</span>
<span class="hljs-comment"># Backend: transformers</span>
ocr = PaddleOCR(
    use_doc_orientation_classify=<span class="hljs-literal">False</span>,
    use_doc_unwarping=<span class="hljs-literal">False</span>,
    use_textline_orientation=<span class="hljs-literal">False</span>,
    engine=<span class="hljs-string">"transformers"</span>,
)
result = ocr.predict(<span class="hljs-string">"https://paddle-model-ecology.bj.bcebos.com/paddlex/imgs/demo_image/general_ocr_002.png"</span>)
</code></pre>
<p>ONNX variants are also available in the <a href="https://huggingface.co/collections/PaddlePaddle/pp-ocrv6">PP-OCRv6 Collection</a> for environments that use ONNX Runtime through <code>engine="onnxruntime"</code>:</p>
<pre><code class="language-Python"><span class="hljs-keyword">from</span> paddleocr <span class="hljs-keyword">import</span> PaddleOCR

<span class="hljs-comment"># Model: PP-OCRv6_medium(Default)</span>
<span class="hljs-comment"># Backend: ONNX Runtime</span>
ocr = PaddleOCR(
    use_doc_orientation_classify=<span class="hljs-literal">False</span>,
    use_doc_unwarping=<span class="hljs-literal">False</span>,
    use_textline_orientation=<span class="hljs-literal">False</span>,
    engine=<span class="hljs-string">"onnxruntime"</span>,
)
result = ocr.predict(<span class="hljs-string">"https://paddle-model-ecology.bj.bcebos.com/paddlex/imgs/demo_image/general_ocr_002.png"</span>)
</code></pre>
<p>Together, these backend options make PP-OCRv6 available across different runtime environments while keeping the same OCR model family on the Hugging Face Hub.</p>
<hr>
<h2 class="relative group flex items-baseline">
	<a id="conclusion" class="block pr-1.5 text-lg md:absolute md:p-1.5 md:opacity-0 md:group-hover:opacity-100 md:right-full" href="#conclusion" rel="nofollow">
		<span class="header-link"> </span>
	</a>
	<span>
		Conclusion
	</span>
</h2>
<p>PP-OCRv6 extends PaddleOCR with a lightweight, multilingual OCR model family for real-world text detection and recognition.</p>
<p>The release includes three model tiers from <strong>1.5M to 34.5M parameters</strong>, up to <strong>50-language OCR support</strong>, improved detection and recognition accuracy over PP-OCRv5_server, and multiple model formats on the Hugging Face Hub, including <strong>safetensors</strong>, <strong>Paddle inference models</strong>, and <strong>ONNX models</strong>.</p>
<p>Together with the hosted Hugging Face Space and the available PaddleOCR inference backends, PP-OCRv6 provides several entry points for evaluation and integration:</p>
<ul>
<li><p><strong>Online Demo</strong>: <a href="https://huggingface.co/spaces/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv6_Online_Demo">PP-OCRv6 Online Demo</a></p>
</li>
<li><p><strong>Model Collection</strong>: <a href="https://huggingface.co/collections/PaddlePaddle/pp-ocrv6">PP-OCRv6 Collection</a></p>
</li>
<li><p><strong>Transformers Backend Blog</strong>: <a href="https://huggingface.co/blog/PaddlePaddle/paddleocr-transformers">PaddleOCR with Transformers Backend</a></p>
</li>
<li><p><strong>PaddleOCR Documentation</strong>: <a href="https://www.paddleocr.ai/latest/en/version3.x/algorithm/PP-OCRv6/PP-OCRv6.html" rel="nofollow">PP-OCRv6 Documentation</a></p>
</li>
<li><p><strong>PaddleOCR Official Website</strong>: <a href="https://www.paddleocr.com" rel="nofollow">https://www.paddleocr.com</a></p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>You can evaluate PP-OCRv6 with the online demo, explore the available model assets in the Collection, and use the inference backend that matches your own OCR workflow.</p>
